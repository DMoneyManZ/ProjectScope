# Weapon-style preset gallery

There are 30 stock presets: the original six plus the following 24.
These are manually chosen visual designs. They do not identify weapons, estimate
spread, track recoil, or predict bullet impact. An external crosshair can differ
from a game's actual sight alignment.

Page Up/Down cycles in sidebar order. Names are grouped by ID, so Pistol01–06,
Shotgun01–06, SMG01–06 and Sniper01–06 stay together. Personal saved presets are
also included. The brief corner label shows the preset name and saved RGB values
on a 0–255 scale. With inversion enabled, those RGB values describe the saved
color; the displayed inverted silhouette depends on the background.

| 06 | Tiny dot | Fine cross | Bold cross + dot | Open fine cross |

The same numbered variants use the same palette across groups: cyan, white,
green, yellow, pink, orange. Each starts with a black outline and 90% opacity.
Use the Appearance controls to adjust any shape or color, then Save as preset.
Pause randomizes, Insert saves and End alternates light/dark colors. Randomize generates additional designs and keeps your chosen opacity.

See preset-gallery.png for all 24 new designs drawn with the actual renderer,
enlarged twice for readability. The gallery shows ordinary color mode; inversion
is a separate session setting and does not rewrite the preset JSON.
